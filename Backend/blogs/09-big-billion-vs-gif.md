## First: When Do These Sales Happen?

Both events run in October, timed to Navratri/Dussehra/Diwali — the biggest gifting season in India.

**Big Billion Days (Flipkart):** Typically 5–7 days in early-to-mid October. Flipkart has historically launched a day before Amazon's sale begins, triggering a price war from hour one.

**Great Indian Festival (Amazon):** Typically starts within 24 hours of Big Billion Days and runs for 7–10 days. Amazon's event is longer and the deals change daily — products cycle in and out, so checking daily is genuinely worthwhile.

Both platforms do "early access" previews 24–48 hours before the official start for their subscription members (Prime and Flipkart Plus respectively). These early access windows are real — some high-demand products sell out at the deal price before the public sale begins.

## The Head-to-Head: Category by Category

### Smartphones — No Clear Winner; Compare Both Every Time

This is the most contested category and the most worth comparing carefully. Both platforms have exclusive brand partnerships that change annually:

- Some Redmi models launch exclusively on Flipkart
- Many OnePlus and Samsung models see their steepest discounts on Amazon
- Poco has historically been Flipkart-exclusive at launch

During the October overlap, both platforms undercut each other on non-exclusive phones. The ₹2,000+ price differences on mid-range phones between platforms are real and documented every year.

**Strategy:** For the specific phone you want, check both apps on Day 1 of the sale and set a price alert for any further drops. Don't assume your historically cheaper platform will win on your specific model.

### Laptops — Amazon Typically Edges Out

Amazon's broader seller network in the laptop category means more seller competition and more opportunities for genuine discounts beyond the brand's own pricing. During Great Indian Festival:

- ASUS, HP, and Lenovo laptops see 15–25% off regularly
- Education editions and older-generation configs get the deepest cuts
- Additional bank cashback on laptop purchases compounds effectively here

Flipkart competes on specific Dell and Lenovo models where it has direct brand partnerships, but Amazon tends to win at the aggregate category level.

**Pro tip:** Laptop prices during GIF tend to be lower than at any other point in the year — including Republic Day and Independence Day sales. If you need a laptop and can wait until October, wait.

### Smart TVs — October Is the Year's Best, Whichever Platform

Both platforms run their absolute deepest TV discounts during October. The LG, Samsung, Sony, and Mi TV discounts during this period are not replicated at any other time of year.

**Brand-specific guidance:**

- LG TVs: Both platforms, compare before buying
- Samsung TVs: Amazon often has Samsung's official store in the Buy Box with brand-backed prices
- Mi/Redmi TVs: Flipkart (historical exclusive sale partner for Xiaomi India)
- Sony Bravia: Amazon often shows better prices due to broader reseller competition

For anything above 43 inches, the savings in October can be ₹5,000–₹15,000 over the rest-of-year price. Don't buy a large TV in March and regret it in October.

### Audio (Headphones, Earbuds, Speakers) — Split by Segment

**Premium audio (Sony, Bose, Sennheiser, JBL):** Amazon is the natural home for these brands due to stronger international brand presence. Sony WH-1000XM series, Bose QuietComfort, and Sennheiser models reliably hit their yearly lows during GIF.

**Budget audio (boAt, Noise, Mivi, OnePlus Buds):** Flipkart competes aggressively here, often with exclusive sale pricing on boAt — which has its strongest commercial relationship with Flipkart.

### Fashion — Go to Myntra, Not These Two

Both Amazon and Flipkart run fashion sales in October, but neither comes close to Myntra's End of Reason Sale (which often coincides) for brand selection and fashion-specific discount depth. For clothing, shoes, and accessories: Myntra every time.

### Large Appliances — Both Are Good, With Different Offers

Refrigerators, washing machines, and ACs all see meaningful discounts in October. The exchange value for old appliances is typically highest during these sales.

Key differentiator: Installation and extended warranty packages. Flipkart's installation offers are bundled for certain appliances and can save ₹500–₹1,000. Amazon's extended warranty third-party packages (via Acko, etc.) are sometimes better on higher-end appliances. Compare the full package, not just the MRP discount.

### Books — Amazon, Always

Amazon has unmatched book inventory in India. Offers during GIF on books are not the deepest in the category but Amazon is simply the dominant platform for this. Flipkart's book section has reduced in priority in recent years.

## What Genuinely Gets Discounted vs What Looks Discounted

The October sales attract a specific type of seller behaviour: inflating the MRP in the weeks before the sale so the "discount" looks dramatic. This is well-documented and annually repeated.

Signs that a "sale" price is real:

- Price was meaningfully higher in August and September
- The discount percentage is in line with what's seen on the product category (15–25% for electronics, not 60–70% unless it's genuinely end-of-life stock)
- Multiple competing sellers converge on a similar "sale" price (suggests genuine market pressure rather than MRP manipulation)

Signs the "discount" is inflated:

- MRP was raised 3 weeks before the sale on price tracking tools
- The product has "never" actually sold at the original price
- Discount exceeds 50% on a product that launched less than 6 months ago

## The Optimal October Strategy

### Before the Sale Starts (1 Week Prior)

1. Build your wishlist on both Amazon and Flipkart for every product you're considering
2. Note current prices so you can calculate the actual discount when the sale begins
3. Sign up for Amazon Prime and Flipkart Plus early access if the annual fee is worth it for you

### Day 1 of the Sale

1. Check your wishlist items on both platforms immediately — genuine deals on popular products go fast
2. For smartphones specifically: compare Amazon and Flipkart on the exact same model
3. Apply your bank's offer before comparing — the effective price changes significantly

### During the Sale (Days 2–7)

1. Amazon refreshes Lightning Deals daily — revisit the deals page every morning
2. Some prices drop further midway through the sale as platforms respond to competitor pricing
3. Don't panic-buy on Day 1 if the discount isn't compelling — prices can fall further on Days 3–5

### After the Sale Ends

1. Post-sale "clearance" pricing on remaining stock sometimes appears 2–3 days after the official end
2. Prices typically rise within 1 week of the sale closing — don't wait if you see a good deal on Day 6 or 7

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Should I buy on Day 1 or wait?**
For very high-demand products (flagship phones like the iPhone during GIF, top-selling TWS earbuds): Day 1 or early access if possible, because stock at deal price can exhaust quickly. For appliances and non-flagship electronics: you have room to wait and compare further into the sale.

**Can prices drop below the sale price using coupons?**
Yes. Bank offers, coupon codes, and exchange bonuses stack with the sale price. Calculate your full loaded discount, not just the listed sale price.

**What if the same product has a sale price on both platforms that's identical?**
Then choose based on seller/delivery reliability and which bank offer applies. The sale price convergence happens sometimes — it usually means the brand itself has set a minimum floor price for the event.

**Are extended warranty plans during October sales worth it?**
For electronics above ₹15,000 — generally yes. The incremental cost of a 2-year extended plan during a sale is lower than buying it afterwards, and the peace of mind on a ₹50,000 TV or laptop has calculable value. Check whether the plan is from a reputable provider (Acko, Croma Protect, Onsitego) rather than an unknown third party.
